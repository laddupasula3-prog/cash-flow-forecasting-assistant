Cash flow forecasting assistant

Enter your opening cash, expected receipts and payments. The app builds a month-by-month forecast with base, downside and upside scenarios, and runs data checks and cash alerts in code, so the numbers are exact. Claude then writes the review using the rules in a written Skill (SKILL.md).
